\section*{S1 Note. Scope of claims}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{S1 Note. Scope of claims}
Throughout, numerical results (baseline 0.0018\%, maximum single-barrier gain 0.0054\%, three-way share 87.6\%, and all Shapley/Sobol/Morris/bootstrap outputs) are \textbf{level-2} model illustrations generated from the chosen topology and parameterization; they are consistent with, but not independent confirmation of, Propositions~1--2 (level~1). Pass probabilities (Table~S-Deriv) are \textbf{level-3} provisional calibration assumptions. Statements about real recourse behavior or policy are \textbf{level-4} hypotheses.

At $\rho=0.5$ the best single-layer intervention (4.50\%) remains far below coordinated removal (96.8\% total), so the comparative/threshold conclusion holds across the correlation sweep; under repeated attempts it attenuates, bounding the model's validity to approximately single-pass serial recourse.
